Put your Commander Keen 4 files in this folder:

  KEEN4E.EXE   (version 1.4, EGA)
  AUDIO.CK4, EGAGRAPH.CK4, GAMEMAPS.CK4 and the other .CK4 files

They are not included: copy them from your own copy of the game. The Steam
release of "Commander Keen" has them in steamapps/common/Commander Keen/base4.
